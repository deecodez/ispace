Story
THE THINKING BEHIND iSPACE

Built to eliminate agency fluff

Good ideas need room to be questioned, the right people to develop them, and clarity to move from possibility to action. The Village is how we cut the agency fluff and build the environment every problem and every business deserves.

The Problem We Saw

Good thinking grows through exchange

The market is crowded with agencies that sound impressive but deliver little strategic value. Different experiences produce different perspectives, so we are built around sharing, challenging, and building on one another's thinking.

The Opportunity

There is more than one way forward

Businesses face questions that rarely fit into one discipline. A marketing challenge might begin with positioning, a technology problem might need a fresh read on the customer. This is why iSpace exists. We look across disciplines to find the answer that fits.

The Village Solution

We bring the right thinking into the room

iSpace brings people, disciplines, and ideas together to help businesses understand their challenges, make better decisions, and build with purpose. The Village is how we do that work. Collaboratively, curiously, and always looking at what becomes possible next.

ROOTED IN CONNECTION

Registered in Canada. Operating globally.

Registered in Calgary, our village spans North America, Latin America, and West Africa. Working across industries, cultures, and business realities shapes how we think, the questions we ask, and the solutions we build.

The Village in practice

Building beyond client work.

The Village isn't just an idea behind our brand. It comes to life through the conversations we start and the work we do alongside people and businesses. Here are a few of the ways we put it into practice.

01 Townhall Masterclasses

Knowledge grows when it is shared.

Learn, exchange ideas, and build practical skills together.

The Village's Townhall Masterclasses create opportunities for people to learn, exchange ideas, and develop practical skills with others. It builds on iSpace's commitment to making useful knowledge accessible and creating room for people to grow through shared learning.

Our Marketing Masterclass brought participants together for focused learning with Coach Ayo Shonibare and helped lay the foundation for a community of emerging marketers.

Explore the Village Masterclass ↗
Townhall Masterclasses
02 Project Give Back 30

Good business deserves good thinking.

Consultation, strategic direction, and professional support for 30 businesses per cycle.

Project Give Back 30 is iSpace's initiative to give back to businesses through consultation, strategic direction, and other professional support. Across each cycle, we work with 30 businesses, helping them examine their challenges, think through opportunities, and gain perspectives they can use to move forward.

It is one way we put our belief into practice: knowledge and experience become more valuable when they are shared in ways that help others make progress.

Discover Project Give Back 30 ↗

03 Town Hall Meetings

Bring a question. Leave with more to think about.

Conversations that matter to businesses, professionals, and the wider community.

Through our Town Hall Meetings, we create space for conversations around ideas, questions, and issues that matter to businesses, professionals, and the wider community.

The aim is to exchange perspectives, make room for different voices, and explore possibilities beyond the usual talking points through Q&A sessions.
